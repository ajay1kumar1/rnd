# Shipping Policy

## Order Processing

Orders are processed within **24 hours** on business days (Monday-Friday,
excluding holidays). Orders placed after 2 PM ET or on weekends are
processed the next business day. You'll receive an email confirmation with
a tracking number as soon as your order ships.

## Domestic Shipping (US & Canada)

| Method               | Delivery Time      | Cost                        |
|----------------------|---------------------|------------------------------|
| Standard Shipping    | 3-5 business days   | Free on orders over $50; $5.99 otherwise |
| Expedited Shipping   | 1-2 business days   | $14.99                      |
| Overnight Shipping   | Next business day   | $29.99 (order by 12 PM ET)  |

## Carriers

We ship via **UPS, USPS, and FedEx** depending on package size, weight,
and destination. You cannot select a specific carrier at checkout.

## International Shipping

We currently ship only within the United States and Canada. We do not
offer international shipping outside of North America at this time.

## Tracking Your Order

Every order includes a tracking number, available in your shipping
confirmation email and via order lookup. Tracking updates may take up to
24 hours to appear after your label is created.

## Lost or Delayed Packages

If a package shows no movement for **5+ business days** after its expected
delivery date, contact support with your order ID. We will file a trace
with the carrier and, if the package is confirmed lost, send a replacement
or issue a full refund.

## Address Accuracy

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. We are not
responsible for orders delayed or lost due to an incorrect or incomplete
address, though we're happy to help coordinate a reshipment if the package
is returned to us.
