# Warranty Information

We stand behind the quality of everything we sell. Coverage varies by
product category, as outlined below.

## Coverage by Category

| Category                        | Warranty Period |
|----------------------------------|------------------|
| Electronics (monitors, keyboards, speakers, chargers) | 1 year limited |
| Furniture (chairs, desks, desk mats) | 2 years limited |
| Accessories (bags, sleeves, stands) | 90 days limited |

The limited warranty covers **defects in materials and workmanship** under
normal use. It does not cover cosmetic wear, or issues arising after the
warranty period has elapsed.

## What's Not Covered

- Damage from accidents, drops, spills, or misuse.
- Damage from unauthorized repairs or modifications.
- Normal wear and tear (e.g., battery capacity loss over time).
- Products purchased from unauthorized third-party resellers.

## Filing a Warranty Claim

1. Contact support with your order ID, a description of the defect, and
   photos or video if the issue is visible.
2. Our team will review the claim, typically within 2 business days.
3. Approved claims are resolved via **repair, replacement, or store
   credit**, at our discretion, and typically ship within 5 business days.

## Extended Warranty

An optional extended warranty (add one additional year of coverage) can be
purchased at checkout for eligible electronics. Extended warranty claims
follow the same process as the standard warranty above.

## Questions

If you're unsure whether your product is still under warranty, contact
support with your order ID and we'll confirm your coverage status.
