# Returns Policy

We want you to be happy with your purchase. If something isn't right, we
make returns straightforward.

## Eligibility

- Items may be returned within **30 days** of the delivery date shown on
  your order.
- Products must be unused, in their original packaging, and include all
  accessories and documentation.
- Items marked **"Final Sale"** at checkout are not eligible for return or
  exchange.
- Gift cards and downloadable software licenses are non-returnable.

## How to Start a Return

1. Contact support with your order ID and the reason for the return.
2. We'll email a prepaid return shipping label for eligible items within
   the US and Canada.
3. Pack the item securely and drop it off at the specified carrier.
4. You'll receive an email confirmation once your return is received and
   inspected.

## Refunds

- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within **5-7 business
  days** of us receiving and inspecting the returned item.
- Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to
  our error (wrong item shipped, defective product, etc.).
- A 15% restocking fee may apply to large items (furniture, monitors 30in
  and up) unless the item arrived damaged or defective.

## Exchanges

We do not process direct exchanges. Return the original item for a refund
and place a new order for the replacement to ensure the fastest turnaround.

## Damaged or Defective Items

If your order arrives damaged or defective, contact support within 7 days
of delivery with photos of the item and packaging. We will cover return
shipping and send a replacement or full refund at no additional cost.

## International Returns

Orders shipped outside the US and Canada must be returned at the
customer's expense. Duties and customs fees paid at import are
non-refundable.
