# Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I track my order?**
Use the tracking number sent in your shipping confirmation email, or ask
support for an order status lookup by order ID.

**Can I change or cancel my order?**
Orders can be changed or cancelled within 1 hour of being placed, as long as
they have not yet entered processing. After that, please refer to our
return policy once the order arrives.

**Do you ship internationally?**
Currently we only ship within the US and Canada. See the shipping policy
for details on delivery times and costs.
